ABOUT ME

I am a Full Stack Developer with extensive experience in Operations and Business Development. I recently completed my Certificate in Full Stack Development from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill and developed skills in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, APIs, React, Node.js, SQL, and Tailwind.
